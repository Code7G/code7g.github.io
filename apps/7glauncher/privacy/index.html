Privacy Policy for 7GLauncher

Last updated: November 9, 2026

7GLauncher is an Android home screen made by Code7G. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

If you have questions, contact us at coder.7g@gmail.com.

1. What is collected

Nothing. 7GLauncher has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. The app doesn't have the internet permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere.

The text font is delivered by Google Play services on your device, the same way it is for other Android apps that use Google Fonts. 7GLauncher itself makes no network connection for it.

2. What stays on your device

Your layout, settings, chosen pictures and wallpapers are stored in the app's private storage on your device, which Android keeps apart from other apps. They stay until you remove them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

A backup is created only when you ask for one, and is saved to the place you choose. It contains your layout, settings and wallpapers. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

3. Permissions

  • Notification access (optional, off until you allow it): used only to show notification dots on app icons. The app counts how many notifications each app has. It keeps those counts in memory while it runs, and does not store or send what the notifications say.
  • All files access (optional, only in the version installed directly from the APK, not in the Google Play version): used only so you can browse for icon pictures and choose a slideshow folder. Without it, the system's photo picker is used.
  • Set wallpaper: to apply the wallpaper you choose to the home screen and the lock screen.
  • Expand status bar: to open the notification shade when you use that gesture.
  • Request delete packages: to let you start uninstalling an app from its menu. Android asks you to confirm.

The app can see which apps are installed, because a home screen has to list them. That list stays on your device.

4. Children's privacy

7GLauncher is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone of any age.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

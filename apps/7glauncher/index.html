About

7GLauncher is a home screen for Android that you can change almost completely. Keep several separate setups in one app, play a video behind your icons, sort apps by color and hide the ones you'd rather keep out of sight. You pay once and get all of it: no subscription, no ads, no account, and the app doesn't have the internet permission.

Features

Environments

  • Each environment is a complete launcher with its own pages, dock, drawer, hidden apps, settings and wallpaper
  • Keep one for work and one for the weekend, or copy your main setup

Wallpapers

  • Video wallpapers, animated GIF or WebP images, and slideshows of up to 100 pictures
  • Photos you place yourself, gradients and solid colors
  • A separate lock screen wallpaper or slideshow

Home screen and widgets

  • Grids from 3 by 3 up to 24 by 24, and icons from 50% to 200%
  • Widget stacks you switch with a swipe, freely resized widgets with an optional glass panel
  • Folders with your own picture as the background, seven icon shapes and installed icon packs
  • Notification dots, with or without the count

Drawer, search and focus

  • Sort apps by name or by icon color
  • Search shows app shortcuts, answers sums, and finds contacts if you turn that on
  • Hidden apps that open with a gesture you pick
  • Focus mode: a plain screen with a clock and only the apps you chose
  • An optional feed page with your calendar, what's playing and your most used apps

Also

  • Eighteen gestures on empty home space, each doing what you choose
  • Back up everything to one file
  • Phones and tablets, each with its own layout

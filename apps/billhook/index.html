About

Write a quote on the job, turn it into an invoice with one tap, and send it as a PDF. Billhook is for people who work alone in a trade: plumbers, electricians, cleaners, gardeners, handymen.

Your first 10 documents are free. After that, one in-app purchase removes the limit. There is no subscription. If you never buy, everything you made stays readable and shareable, quotes can still become invoices, and backup and export keep working.

It isn't on Google Play yet.

Features

Quotes and invoices

  • Line items, quantities, discounts, your own tax rates, notes and an expiry date
  • Turn a quote into an invoice with a due date in one tap
  • Invoice numbers run in order and are never reused
  • See which invoices are unpaid or overdue, and mark them sent or paid

Your business

  • Business name, logo, contact details, tax ID and payment instructions on every document
  • A customer list and a price list of the work and materials you charge for

Send and keep

  • Share a PDF through any app on your phone, and change the wording on your documents
  • Back up to a file you choose, restore it on another phone, and export to CSV
  • 31 languages, and documents in the language your customer reads

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't give tax advice: you type your own tax rates
  • It makes PDF documents, not structured e-invoices, which some countries require for some customers
  • It doesn't collect payments from your customers

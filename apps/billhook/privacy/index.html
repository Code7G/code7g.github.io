Privacy Policy for Billhook

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Billhook is an Android app for writing quotes and invoices, made by Code7G, a solo developer ("we", "us"). This policy says what happens to your information when you use it. You don't need an account, and Billhook has no server.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your business details: name, address, phone, email, tax ID, payment instructions, currency and logo
  • Your customers: name, phone, email and address
  • Your price list, and your quotes and invoices with their lines, dates, notes and status
  • Your settings, such as the app language and when you last made a backup

We can't see any of this information. Billhook has no ads and no analytics or crash reporting of its own.

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, this information is gone unless you made a backup. The app reminds you to make one.

2. What you choose to send

When you share a PDF, or save a backup or CSV file, Android asks you where it should go. The file goes only where you send it, and what happens to it there is covered by the app or service you picked. A backup file contains all of the information listed above, including your logo, so keep it somewhere safe.

Your logo is read only when you pick an image, using Android's photo picker. The app has no access to your other photos.

3. Paying through Google Play

Billhook has one optional purchase, which removes the limit on free documents. It is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google receives and processes your purchase under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

To check whether you have bought the unlock and to show the price, the app asks Google Play each time you open it. It does this with Google's Play Billing Library, which is why the app lists internet and network access permissions. The library can send Google technical information about these checks, such as whether a call worked. Google runs that part, and we don't receive or control this information. Billhook's own code doesn't use the internet, and your documents and customer details are never given to the library. The app keeps one note on your phone saying whether the purchase was found, so it still works when you have no signal.

4. Permissions

Billhook's own code asks for no permissions. These are added by Google's Play Billing Library:

  • Billing: to make and check the in-app purchase
  • Internet and network state: for Google Play Billing only

The app has no access to your camera, contacts, location, files or notifications. Files are opened and saved only through Android's own pickers, and only the file you choose.

5. Your rights

Because your information stays on your phone, you control it directly: you can change or delete it in the app, or delete all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. We hold no copy we could give you or delete. For data Google handles for purchases, see Google's privacy policy above. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

Billhook is a business tool and is not meant for children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at this web address, with a new date at the top.

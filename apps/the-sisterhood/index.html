About

The Sisterhood is a fitness tracker made for women: a workout log, calorie and water tracker and step counter. Every set, meal and step earns XP and levels and keeps your streak going.

Features

Made for you

  • A one-minute setup asks your goal, experience, schedule and any health concerns
  • Knees, back, pregnancy and more: risky exercises are swapped for gentler ones or marked
  • Guided workouts: the timer runs each set and rest

Training

  • 200+ exercises with how-to demos, including hip thrusts, glute bridges, lunges and core work
  • Programs such as Glute Builder, Glutes at Home, Strong & Toned, Core & Posture, and your first push-up or pull-up
  • Every workout is planned for you and gets a little harder each week
  • A body map that levels up each muscle group

Food and water

  • Calorie and macro goals to lose, maintain or build
  • 11 weekly food plans: Balanced, Mediterranean, Vegan, High Protein and more
  • 560+ built-in foods, barcode scanning, and plate photos recognised on your phone
  • Water tracking and gentle fasting plans from 12:12 to 16:8

Your cycle (optional)

  • Log your period, spotting, symptoms and mood in a tap
  • Predictions learned from your own cycle, and a perimenopause mode
  • Stays on your phone, is never used for ads, and can be deleted in one tap

Steps and streaks

  • Steps from Health Connect (including your watch) or your phone's step counter
  • Pick your training days; planned rest days keep your streak
  • Streak Freeze, Streak Shield, quests and a Weekly Pact

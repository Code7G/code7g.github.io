Privacy Policy for ReadMD

Last updated: November 17, 2026

ReadMD is a Markdown file viewer for Android and Windows, made by Code7G (a solo developer, contact below).

Short version

  • ReadMD never sends your files, their names, where they are saved or anything you read in them.
  • The Android app can send anonymous usage statistics, only if you tap "Allow" when the app first asks. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • The Windows app sends nothing at all.
  • No ads, no account, no advertising ID, no crash reporting.

Usage statistics (Android only, optional)

When you first open the Android app, it asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. Until you tap "Allow", nothing is collected. If you tap "No thanks", nothing is ever collected. You can change your choice at any time in the app: menu, Settings, "Share anonymous usage statistics".

If you allow them, ReadMD uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google, to count:

  • that the app was started, and whether it was in light or dark mode
  • that a file was opened, and how: the file picker, "Open with", Share, the recent list or a link in another file. Never which file.
  • that a file could not be opened, and the kind of problem (not found, too large, unreadable)
  • that find in page, the contents list or the text size control was used
  • what Google Analytics records on its own for every app: app starts and sessions, the time spent in the app, the app version, the device model, the Android version, the language, the time zone offset, the install source (which app store installed the app), and the country or region (Google works this out from a shortened IP address and does not store the IP address)

Google Analytics gives each installation a random ID (the app instance ID) so it can count users. ReadMD does not link this ID to your name, your Google account or anything else. ReadMD does not collect the advertising ID and does not ask for permission to use it. The app tells Google Analytics that ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are not allowed, and it never changes that, even when you allow the statistics.

Statistics are sent encrypted (TLS). Google processes them as Google Analytics for Firebase, under Google's terms and privacy policy, and keeps them for the period set in Google Analytics (see Google's page on data retention: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196). Depending on the Google Analytics settings, Google may also use them to maintain and improve its own services, as described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We don't sell the statistics and don't give them to anyone other than Google. Turning the statistics off also deletes the app instance ID and any statistics not yet sent from your phone.

Why we collect them: to see which features are used and where opening files goes wrong, so we know what to improve. The legal basis in the EU and UK is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What stays on your device

  • The list of recent files (each file's name and where it is saved), so you can open them again.
  • Your text size setting and your choice about usage statistics.

This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can remove a file from the recent list in the app, and uninstalling ReadMD deletes all of it.

Your files

ReadMD only reads the files you open with it. It never changes, uploads or shares them.

Internet use

The Android app has internet access only for the optional usage statistics above. Documents never load anything from the internet: ReadMD does not load images from the web, and when you tap a link in a document it hands the link to your browser or mail app. What happens after that is covered by that app's privacy policy. The Windows app makes no network connections at all.

Children

ReadMD is a general tool and is not made for children. We don't knowingly collect anything about children.

Your rights

In the EU and UK you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you and to withdraw consent. The statistics are not linked to who you are, so we cannot find "your" statistics from your name or email. The simplest way to stop and remove them is to turn them off in Settings (this deletes the ID on your phone) or uninstall the app; what Google already holds is deleted when its retention period in Google Analytics ends. You can also write to us, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Slovenia: the Information Commissioner, https://www.ip-rs.si).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. If the app starts collecting anything new, it will ask you again first.

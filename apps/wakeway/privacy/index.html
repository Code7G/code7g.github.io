Privacy Policy for WakeWay

Last updated: November 16, 2026

WakeWay is an Android alarm app published by Code7G ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data stays on your device, what leaves it, who receives it and what choices you have. You don't need an account to use the app, and it has no sign-in.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us, and we can't recover it for you:

  • Your alarms, reminders, notes, morning checklist and settings
  • Your streaks, quests, XP, level, Light Gems balance and unlocked items
  • Your profile name, "about me" text and profile picture
  • Videos you choose for your own video alarms, with their thumbnails
  • Sleep data read from Health Connect, see below

Pictures and videos are chosen with Android's photo picker, so the app only gets the one item you pick, and keeps its own copy in its private storage.

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, this data is deleted permanently.

WakeWay has no analytics or crash reporting. We don't receive any record of how you use the app.

Sleep data (Health Connect, optional)

If you connect Health Connect on the Sleep tab, the app reads the sleep sessions and sleep stages that your watch or sleep app saved there. It reads them only while the app is open and never writes anything to Health Connect.

This data is used only to show your sleep, calculate your sleep score and support the bedtime reminder. Sleep data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

You can stop this at any time on the Sleep tab, or by removing the permission in Health Connect. The app's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data that leaves your device

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between some screens, and rewarded video ads you can choose to watch for Light Gems. Ads are never shown while an alarm rings, during snooze or on the wake-up screen, and not to VIP members or people who bought No Ads. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, device information, app interactions and approximate location derived from your IP address, and may use them to choose, measure and protect ads. We don't have access to, or control over, the data Google collects. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads are requested. It stores your choice on your device and sends Google the information needed to show the form and apply your choice.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases and the VIP subscription are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card number, bank details or billing address. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and uses it on your device to unlock what you bought, and asks Google Play whether a subscription is still active. See the Google Play terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

Fonts (Google Play services)

Some fonts used in the app, such as clock styles, are downloaded through Google Play services the first time they are needed. The app sends only the font's name.

All of these services are operated by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone other than these services, except where the law requires it. Google may process this data on servers outside your own country, under its data protection terms.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Google's own retention rules apply to data collected through AdMob, the consent form and Google Play.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: change your consent choice under "Privacy Options" in the app's Settings.
  • Ad personalization: limit it or reset your Advertising ID in your device's settings (on most Android devices, under Google > Ads or Privacy > Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Health Connect: disconnect on the Sleep tab or in Health Connect.
  • Permissions: review or remove them in your device's settings. Alarms may stop working reliably without the required ones.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time. The data the app keeps is on your device, where you can change or delete it yourself. For anything else, contact us at the address below. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on your consent for advertising and on performing our contract with you for purchases, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law sets one. We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us.

Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit, and data on your device is kept in the app's private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Permissions

The app requests these permissions, and doesn't use them to collect personal data:

  • Exact alarms: to ring at the times you set
  • Boot completed: to set your alarms again after a restart or update
  • Full-screen intent: to show the alarm when the device is locked
  • Notifications, vibration, wake lock and foreground service (media playback): so alarms can ring and play their sound
  • Display over other apps (optional): to open the alarm screen when an alarm goes off while you are using your phone. Nothing is drawn over other apps.
  • Health Connect sleep data (optional): read-only, for the Sleep tab
  • Internet and network state: for ads, the consent form, purchases and fonts
  • Advertising ID and Android's ad services permissions: added by Google AdMob, used for ads as described above
  • Billing: for purchases through Google Play

The app has no access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For a significant change, we may also give notice in the app or on the Play Store listing.

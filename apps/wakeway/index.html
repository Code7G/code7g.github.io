About

WakeWay is an alarm app for Android built around one idea: you pick how your morning starts. Choose the sound, the look of the ring screen, how you turn the alarm off and what happens after you get up, from a short breathing exercise to a morning checklist.

It isn't on Google Play yet.

Features

Alarms

  • Exact alarms that ring over the lock screen and come back after a restart
  • Tap the buttons, or swipe a handle up to wake or snooze
  • A different "I'm up" sound and snooze sound for each alarm
  • Home screen widgets for your next alarm, streak and sleep score

Sound and look

  • 55 sounds made by the app in 9 groups, from gentle and nature to retro and space, plus your phone's own alarm and ringtone sounds
  • Favorites and shuffle
  • Clock faces, ambient effects and alarm cards, matching sets, and a preview of the ring screen before you save
  • Your own video as an alarm

After you wake up

  • A morning note, your own checklist, one minute of guided breathing or four gentle stretches, or all of them as a full morning routine
  • Reminders with your own text, right after waking or up to two hours later, and a heads-up before bed
  • Streaks, quests and levels, and Light Gems to unlock more sounds, looks and routines

Sleep

  • Plan your bedtime from your alarm and get a sleep score
  • Reads your sleep from Health Connect if you want to (optional, read only, stays on your phone)

About

WakeWay is an alarm app for Android built around one idea: you pick how your morning starts. Choose the sound, the look of the ring screen, how you turn the alarm off and what happens after you get up, from a short breathing exercise to a morning checklist.

It isn't on Google Play yet.

Features

Alarms

  • Exact alarms that ring over the lock screen and come back after a restart
  • Snooze, and a Lock Alarm option for mornings you can't miss
  • Tap or swipe to turn an alarm off, set per alarm
  • Home screen widgets for your next alarm, streak and sleep score

Sound and look

  • 55 sounds in 9 categories, plus your phone's own alarm and ringtone sounds
  • Favorites and shuffle
  • Clocks, backgrounds and cards for the ring screen
  • Your own video as an alarm

After you wake up

  • A morning note, a checklist, one minute of breathing and gentle stretches
  • Quests, streaks, levels and achievements

Sleep

  • Reads your sleep from Health Connect, if you connect it
  • Sleep score for last night and the week, and a bedtime reminder

Privacy Policy for Motivational Alarm

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Motivational Alarm is an Android app published by Code7G ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data stays on your device, what leaves it, who receives it and what choices you have. You don't need an account to use the app, and it has no sign-in.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us, and we can't recover it for you:

  • Your alarms, reminders, settings and sleep preferences
  • Your streaks, quests, XP, Motivation balance and unlocked items
  • Your profile name, bio and profile picture
  • Videos you choose for "Create your own" alarms. The name you give such an alarm is the one exception, see "Usage analytics" below.

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, this data is deleted permanently.

Sleep data (Health Connect, optional)

If you connect Health Connect on the Sleep tab, the app reads the sleep sessions and sleep stages (light, deep, REM and awake time) that your watch or sleep app saved there. It reads them only while the app is open and never writes anything to Health Connect.

This data is used only to show your sleep, calculate your sleep score and predict tonight's score. The app saves your usual bedtime, worked out from these nights, so that bedtime reminders can show the same predicted score. Sleep data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

You can stop this at any time with the Health Connect switch on the Sleep tab, or by removing the permission in Health Connect. The app's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data that leaves your device

Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use this to see how the app is used, so we can improve it. It collects a Firebase-generated app instance identifier, device and app information (such as device model, Android version, language and app version), approximate location derived from your IP address, and events from inside the app. Those events include:

  • Alarms being created, dismissed or snoozed, with the snooze count
  • Quests, streaks, onboarding steps and permission choices
  • Screens opened, offers shown, ads shown and purchases started or completed
  • Motivation earned or spent, and your balance as a rough range
  • Whether VIP or No Ads is active
  • Promo codes being redeemed
  • The name of an alarm preset you preview or assign. This includes a name you typed yourself for a "Create your own" alarm, so please don't put personal information in those names.

We can see this data in the Firebase console. It is not linked to your real name or email address, because we don't collect them.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

We use these to find and fix bugs. A report contains the crash details, an installation identifier, device information (such as model, Android version and available memory) and the state of the app at the time of the crash. We can see these reports in the Firebase console.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which helps keep the app free. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from your IP address, and may use them to choose ads. We don't have access to, or control over, the ad-targeting data Google collects. See https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card number, bank details or billing address. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and uses it on your device to unlock what you bought. See the Google Play terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

Preset downloads (Google Play)

Some alarm videos are downloaded through Google Play when you need them.

Promo codes (Google Cloud Firestore)

Only if you redeem a promo code, the app sends that code and a one-way hash made from your device's Android ID, manufacturer and model to our Firestore database, where it is stored with the time of redemption. We keep this so that one device can't redeem the same code twice. Reinstalling the app doesn't reset it.

All of these services are operated by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Firebase terms (https://firebase.google.com/terms). We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone other than these services, except where the law requires it.

Google may process this data on servers in the United States and other countries outside your own, under Google's data protection terms and their transfer safeguards.

How long data is kept

Analytics data and crash reports are kept for Google's default Firebase retention periods. Google's own retention rules apply to data collected through AdMob and Google Play.

Your choices

  • Analytics and crash reports: turn off "Share usage analytics" or "Share crash reports" in the app's Settings at any time.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are shown a consent form before ads, analytics and crash reporting begin. You can change your choice under "Privacy Options" in the app's Settings.
  • Ad personalization: limit it or reset your Advertising ID in your device's settings (on most Android devices, under Google > Ads or Privacy > Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Permissions: review or remove them in your device's settings. Alarms may stop working reliably without the required ones.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, contact us at the address below. Most of the data we can reach is tied to an identifier rather than your name, so we may need you to help us find it, and in some cases we may not be able to.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on your consent for analytics, crash reports and advertising, on performing our contract with you for purchases, and on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse for promo code records. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in California or another US state with a similar law: we don't sell personal information for money. Giving Advertising IDs to Google for personalized ads may count as "sharing" under those laws, and you can opt out using the ad and consent settings above.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law sets one. We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us.

Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit, and data on your device is kept in the app's private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Permissions

The app requests these permissions, and doesn't use them to collect personal data:

  • Exact alarms: to ring at the times you set
  • Boot completed: to restore alarms after a restart
  • Full-screen intent: to show alarms when the device is locked
  • Notifications, vibration, wake lock and foreground service: to make sure alarms can alert you
  • Internet and network state: for ads, analytics, crash reports, purchases, downloads and promo codes
  • Display over other apps (optional): to open the alarm screen when an alarm goes off while you are using your phone. Nothing is drawn over other apps.
  • Health Connect sleep data (optional): read-only, for the Sleep tab, see "Sleep data" above

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For a significant change, we may also give notice in the app or on the Play Store listing.

Motivational Alarm

Wake up motivated with alarms that prepare you for success.

About

When your alarm goes off, Motivational Alarm plays a full-screen motivating video. You hear the same message every morning, and that repetition helps it stick. Getting up instead of snoozing earns you Motivation and XP and extends your streak.

Features

Wake up

  • Full-screen video alarms from a growing library, downloaded only when you pick one
  • No Snooze Mode makes dismissing deliberately harder
  • Lock Alarm freezes an alarm's time and days for up to 7 days
  • A background service rings with the screen on, off or locked, with a fallback if a video fails to load

Your alarms

  • Repeat days, looping and vibration control
  • Sleep-time targets
  • After Wake-Up reminders that show your first task the moment you dismiss
  • Skins, clock faces, fonts, sounds and effects for each alarm

Rewards

  • Motivation to spend in the shop
  • XP and levels that unlock themes and titles
  • Quests for early wake-ups, no-snooze runs and streaks
  • A Streak Freeze that saves a missed day
  • A Wake-Up Rate that shows how often you really get up

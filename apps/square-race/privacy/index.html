Privacy Policy for Square Race

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Square Race is a free-to-play game for Android with optional in-app purchases, published by Code7G. Most of the game (races, levels, the level editor, your unlocks and your Gems balance) runs and stays entirely on your device. This policy explains the data that leaves the device and why.

If you have questions, contact us at coder.7g@gmail.com.

1. Data collection

The app itself does not collect account information, and does not require you to create an account or sign in. Your progress, unlocks, cosmetics, Gems balance and any custom levels you build in the level editor are stored on your device and are not sent to the developer.

The app uses the following third-party services, which do collect data as described below.

Google Firebase Analytics: used to understand how the app is used in aggregate (for example which tracks are played, how races end, which shop items are bought and how often the level editor is used), so we can improve reliability and the core experience. It collects app interaction data and device and app identifiers (such as a Firebase-generated app instance identifier). This data is pseudonymous: it is not linked to your name, email or other directly identifying information, because we do not collect that information in the first place. The developer sees this aggregated data in the Firebase console.

Google Firebase Crashlytics: used to receive crash and error reports so we can find and fix bugs. It collects crash logs and diagnostic information (for example device model, OS version and the state the app was in when it crashed). The developer sees this data in the Firebase console.

Both services are operated by Google and governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Firebase's terms (https://firebase.google.com/terms).

2. Advertising

The app shows interstitial and rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer does not have access to or control over ad-targeting data collected by Google through AdMob.

3. In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases of Gems, the in-game currency, processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details (card number, billing address and so on) are handled by Google Play and are never seen or stored by the developer. The developer only receives confirmation that a purchase completed, so the matching Gems can be added to your save on your device.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form before ads and analytics data collection begin, so you can choose whether to consent.

5. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Data retention

Data collected through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is kept according to Google's default Firebase retention settings, and is not sold or shared with anyone other than Google/Firebase as our service provider.

7. Your choices

You can limit ads personalization in your device's ad settings (Google Settings > Ads, on most Android devices). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can change your consent choice at any time with the "Privacy Options" button on the app's main menu.

8. Permissions

The app uses the following permissions, which are added automatically by the third-party services above rather than requested by our own code:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, sync purchases, and send analytics and crash data.
  • Billing: to process in-app Gems purchases through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to serve ads and, where you consented, personalize them.
  • Wake lock, foreground service and install referrer: standard requirements of the Google Play Billing and AdMob SDKs.

None of these permissions are used by the developer to collect or send personal data beyond what is described above.

9. Contact

For questions or support: coder.7g@gmail.com

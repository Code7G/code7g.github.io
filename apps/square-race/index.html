Square Race

Aim, launch and smash through 240 physics levels in 8 worlds.

Android

About

Pull back, aim and launch your square through glass, ice, portals, crates and bosses. Square Race is a physics game with a story mode, eight worlds, a level editor and a race mode where you sit back and watch the chaos.

The story

Red was hanging out in Square Plaza when Orange showed up and wrecked the party. Red escaped through a portal, lost his memory on the way, and now has to fight his way home level by level. In Season 2 he lands in the Circle Dimension, where Red Circle has banned corners, edges and squares.

Features

Play

  • 240 levels across 8 worlds, plus secret levels and 9 bonus levels in the Crystal Dimension
  • An aim guide that shows where your shot will bounce
  • Lasers, portals, gravity wells, turrets, springs, ice, glass and more
  • Boss levels, and OVERKILL levels with chain explosions
  • Power-ups, with free ones every 3 days

Watch

  • Pick a track and how many squares race, then watch them bounce to the finish
  • Every race has a seed code, so you can replay the same race or send it to a friend

Build

  • A level editor with every piece from the game
  • Play your level right away, and export or import levels as files

Make it yours

  • Hundreds of skins, plus blasts, finish lines and sound packs
  • Square Plaza: rebuild 16 buildings for permanent perks
  • Daily streak rewards and limited-time events
  • Portrait or landscape, on phones and tablets

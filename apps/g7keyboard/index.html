About

A keyboard sees everything you type. G7Keyboard is built so that what you type stays on your phone: the app doesn't have the internet permission, so it has no way to send your keystrokes, clipboard or anything else to a server. There's no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.

What the keyboard does save, like your clipboard history and custom key layouts, is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key kept in the Android Keystore. While your phone is locked, nothing can decrypt that data, including the keyboard itself. It's also left out of Android's cloud backup.

Features

Privacy

  • No internet permission, so nothing can be sent anywhere
  • Encrypted clipboard history you can review and clear
  • Key scramble: shuffles which character each key types, so someone watching your fingers or a screen recording can't tell what you wrote
  • Nothing goes into Android's cloud backup

Typing

  • 30 languages, each with its own layout, including Korean (Hangul), Japanese (Kana) and Chinese (Pinyin with word and phrase prediction)
  • Automatic capitals at the start of a sentence
  • Number pad and the right Enter key for number, phone and date fields
  • An optional PC keys row with Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt and arrows

Layout

  • Compact keyboard and one-hand mode
  • Landscape and tablet layouts
  • Math and symbol keyboards

Make it yours

  • Built-in themes, or design your own with per-key colours and your own background image (kept on your device)
  • Emoji picker with recents and search, text faces and stylised fonts
  • Vibration and key animations you can turn on or off separately

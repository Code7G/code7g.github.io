About

Most plans fail on the days you don't show up. The Brotherhood is a workout log, calorie and water tracker and step counter that turns every set, meal and step into XP, levels, quests and a streak worth protecting.

Features

Built around you

  • A one-minute setup asks your goal, experience, schedule and any injuries
  • Your plan, calories and recommended program adapt to your answers
  • With bad knees or a bad back, risky exercises are swapped for safer ones or marked
  • Guided workouts: the timer runs each set and rest

Training

  • 190+ exercises with looping how-to demos, or add your own
  • Your last numbers are filled in, so you know what to beat
  • Personal records, celebrated when you set them
  • Programs for your first pull-up, strength, muscle, abs, fat loss or home training
  • A body map that levels up each muscle group

Food and water

  • Calorie and macro goals for cutting, maintaining or bulking
  • 11 weekly food plans with a shopping list
  • 560+ built-in foods, barcode scanning and online search
  • Snap your plate: the food is recognised on your phone, nothing is uploaded
  • Water tracking and a fasting timer

Steps and streaks

  • Steps from Health Connect (including your watch) or your phone's step counter
  • Cardio from the Steps screen, no location needed
  • Pick your training days; planned rest days keep your streak
  • Streak Freeze, Streak Shield, quests and a Weekly Pact

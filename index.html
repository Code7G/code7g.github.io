How the apps are made

No accounts

None of the apps need an account. Your alarms, workouts and progress are stored on your device.

Bugs before features

An alarm has to ring and a workout log can't lose a set, so bug fixes come before new features.

Regular updates

Square Race has had more than 30 releases, and the other apps get new features and fixes too.
